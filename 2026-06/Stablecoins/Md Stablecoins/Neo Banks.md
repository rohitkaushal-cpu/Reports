How Neobanks Make Money

Neobanks generate revenue through four primary streams:

1. Interchange Fees

Every time a customer swipes their debit or credit card, the merchant pays
a 1-3% interchange fee. The card network (Visa/Mastercard) takes a cut,
and the neobank keeps the rest. This is the biggest revenue driver (Chime
derives approximately 70-90% of its revenue from interchange alone).

2. Lending Products

Credit cards, personal loans, and buy-now-pay-later products generate
interest income. Traditional banks make 50-60% of revenue from lending,
but neobanks start with deposits and cards, adding lending once they have
customer trust and data.

Nubank’s credit portfolio reached approximately $21 billion by Q3
2024, with reported ROE reaching the high-20s to approximately 30%,
significantly higher than traditional banks (typically 15-18%) because they
target underserved customers and maintain tight risk management.

3. Premium Subscriptions

Monthly fees ($10-45) for premium features like airport lounge access,
better FX rates, and crypto trading. Revolut (Revolut Premium) and Nubank
(Nubank Ultravioleta) both monetize premium tiers successfully. This is
predictable, high-margin recurring revenue.

4. FX & Crypto Trading

Spreads on currency exchanges and also crypto trades now. Revolut
supports 80+ cryptocurrencies and saw strong growth in Wealth/Crypto
revenues during 2024. Especially lucrative in LATAM, where customers
constantly convert volatile local currencies to dollars or stablecoins.

purchases, paid in USDC. Make it transparent, instant, and real.
Users will choose your card over traditional options if the reward
is better and the UX is good. Combine this with loyalty points that
unlock higher tiers, better yields, or exclusive perks.

Most Web3 projects waste ad budgets on cold traffic. Retargeting
is where the ROI happens. Someone visited your landing page
but didn’t sign up? Retarget them with social proof (testimonials,
user counts, security features). Someone downloaded the app but
didn’t complete KYC? Show them a video of the process taking
60 seconds. Someone completed KYC but hasn’t funded? Offer a
