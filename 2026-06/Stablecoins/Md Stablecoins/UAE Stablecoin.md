www.neoslegal.co#1 THE REGIME
IS LIVE

The UAE has three different dedicated
frameworks for stablecoin issuance:

UAE Central Bank’s Payment Tokens
VARA’s Category 1 Fiat-Referenced Virtual
Assets (FRVAs)
ADGM’s Fiat- Referenced Tokens (FRTs)

STABLECOIN ISSUANCE IS A REGULATED
ACTIVITIY AND LICENSING IS MANDATORY.

www.neoslegal.co#2 AED-FIRST
PAYMENTS

Onshore retail payments are designed to be
dirham-centric. Dirham-pegged
stablecoins serve as the intended
means for everyday transactions.

Stablecoins denominated in foreign
currencies have limited applications onshore,
primarily confined to virtual-asset purchases.

ISSUANCE AND SERVICING OF ALGORITHMIC
STABLECOINS ARE NOT PERMITTED IN THE UAE.

www.neoslegal.co#3 TRANSITION
PERIOD IS OVER

From August 2025, merchants are only allowed
to receive crypto payments for goods and
services   in approved AED payment tokens.

This brings to a close the
transition period established
by the Central Bank in
August 2024.

www.neoslegal.co#4 REGULATORY
EXPECTATIONS
ARE HIGH

Stablecoin issuance rules across all UAE
jurisdictions mandate:

sufficient reserves,

adequate liquid funds,

routine audits, disclosures, and

transparent redemption rights.

www.neoslegal.co#5 VARA’S UNIQUE
APPROACH:
ARVA & FRVA

ARVA requirements focus
more on reserve
composition, valuation/
rebalancing methodologies,
and risk disclosures.

FRVA issuers are subject
to a tighter standard on
reserves, redemption at par,
disclosures/ attestations,
and governance.

BOTH REQUIRE A CATEGORY 1 (ISSUANCE) LICENCE
