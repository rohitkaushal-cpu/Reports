They think RWA is just about turning a house into an NFT.

It’s not.

In this episode of Trustee Talks, we break down what RWA actually is, how real-world asset tokenization works in practice, and why this sector is becoming one of the most important bridges between traditional finance and crypto.

🎙 Guest: Mariia Yatsenko — Head of Marketing at Centrifuge, one of the earliest and most respected platforms in the RWA space, founded in 2017.

Together, we discuss:
What Real-World Assets really mean (and why RWA ≠ JPEG NFTs)
How companies tokenize real-world assets step by step
What types of assets are typically tokenized — and what doesn’t make sense
Why structure and jurisdiction matter in RWA
What stakeholders are involved when bringing assets on-chain
How Centrifuge works with institutions and regulation
Why RWA is growing so fast right now
The biggest fears, barriers, and misconceptions around RWA
Why marketing in RWA is about trust, not hype
The future of real-world assets on-chain

We also talk about:
Marketing complex financial infrastructure in Web3
Whether RWA is hype — or the foundation of mass adoption

💡 Plus: practical advice for anyone curious about crypto, RWA, and real use cases — without chasing hype or “guaranteed returns”.

If you want to understand how real finance is moving on-chain — this episode is for you.
