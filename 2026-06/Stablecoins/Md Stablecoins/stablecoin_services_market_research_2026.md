# Stablecoin Services: Market Research Report
### Companies, Demand, USA Market & Geographic Hotspots (2025–2026)
**Research Date:** June 19, 2026

---

## MARKET OVERVIEW

- **Total stablecoin market cap**: $312B+ (October 2025), projected to exceed **$1 trillion** by late 2026
- **Transaction volume**: $33 trillion in 2025 — a **72% YoY increase**, monthly peaks hitting $969.9B (August 2025)
- **US crypto transaction volume**: Surpassed $1 trillion in H1 2025 alone (+50%)
- **GENIUS Act**: Signed July 18, 2025 — America's first federal stablecoin framework
- **VC investment**: $1.5B+ flowed into stablecoin infrastructure companies in 2025
- **Key M&A**: Stripe acquired Bridge ($1.1B), Mastercard acquiring BVNK (~$1.8B), Ripple acquired Hidden Road ($1.25B)
- **Stablecoin circulation projected**: $1 trillion by late 2026

## 1. STABLECOIN RAILS

**Companies:** Fireblocks, Circle (Circle Payments Network), Ripple, BVNK, Bridge (Stripe-owned), Conduit, Solana, Ethereum, Tron

**Demand & Market Size:**
- B2B stablecoin payments: <$100M/month (early 2023) → $6B+/month (mid-2025) — 60x increase
- Fireblocks: 15% of all global stablecoin volume, 35M+ transactions/month, $200B+ monthly
- BVNK: $30B annualized volume in 2025 (2.3x growth)

**USA Demand:** 1/3 of BVNK's volume = $10B from US market. Early Warning Services (Zelle parent — BoA, JPMorgan, Wells Fargo) announced stablecoin-based international expansion (Oct 2025). Fiserv announced FIUSD on Solana for 10,000-institution network.

**ICP:** Large banks and payment processors, global corporations with high-volume cross-border needs, fintechs building on programmable settlement rails.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** New York (regulated banking HQs), San Francisco (Coinbase/Ripple/Stripe), Texas (crypto-forward state law), Wyoming (first state stablecoin).

## 2. STABLECOIN ISSUANCE

**Companies:** Tether (USDT, $164B+), Circle (USDC, $74–76B, NYSE IPO June 2025 raising $1.2B), Paxos (USDP, powers PayPal PYUSD $3.6B+), Ethena (USDe $3B+), Fiserv (FIUSD on Solana), SoFi (enterprise stablecoin Dec 2025), Coinbase (white-label issuance), Ondo Finance, PayPal (PYUSD)

**Demand & Market Size:**
- USDC grew 78% YoY in 2025
- USDT monthly processing averaged $703B, peaked at $1.01 trillion (June 2025)
- Stablecoin issuers held ~$155B in US T-bills by October 2025
- McKinsey projects $2–4 trillion market cap by 2030 (CAGR ~40%)

**USA Demand:** GENIUS Act requires issuers >$10B under OCC supervision; 1:1 reserves in HQLAs mandatory. 54% of FIs not yet issuing stablecoins expect to begin by 2026.

**ICP:** Banks wanting branded stablecoins, corporations tokenizing treasury, payment networks seeking settlement finality, neobanks building dollar-denominated global wallets.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** New York (Circle HQ, Paxos, Ondo Finance), San Francisco (Coinbase), Wyoming (FRNT state stablecoin — launched August 2025 on 7 blockchains).

## 5. STABLECOIN REMITTANCE

**Companies:** Western Union (USDPT launching 2026 on Solana), Ripple/RLUSD (invested in Flutterwave at $3.2B valuation, June 2026), Strike, Sling Money, Bitso (Latin America), MoneyGram (on Stellar), Flywire, Yellow Card (Africa), LemFi

**USA Demand:** US = world's largest remittance sender. Key corridors: US→Mexico ($60B+/year traditional), US→Philippines, US→India, US→El Salvador.

**ICP:** US-based immigrant workers (Hispanic, South Asian, Filipino, African), diaspora communities, gig platform workers (DoorDash, Uber drivers), unbanked populations.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** Texas (largest US→Mexico corridor, 11M+ Hispanic immigrants), California (LA, Fresno — Hispanic, Filipino, Central American), Florida (Caribbean, Latin American corridors), New York, Chicago.

## 6. CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS

**Companies:** Ripple (RLUSD + XRP Ledger), Circle (CPN, launched April 2025), BVNK, Bridge (Stripe), Conduit, Fireblocks, Fiserv (FIUSD), JPMorgan (JPM Coin extended to public blockchains Nov 2025), Western Union, Flutterwave (Ripple-backed)

**Demand & Market Size:**

- B2B cross-border payments grew 50x since 2023

**USA Demand:** Fed published research (March 2026) on payment stablecoins' cross-border role. PNC, Citi, Wells Fargo (via Early Warning Services/Zelle) exploring joint stablecoin for international payments.

**ICP:** Multinational corporations with foreign subsidiaries, import/export companies, supply chain finance platforms, global freelancer/contractor platforms (Toptal, Upwork), B2B SaaS billing globally, Fortune 500 treasury departments.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** New York (global bank HQs, trade finance), Miami (Latin America corridor — Orbital expanding June 2026), Texas (Mexico/LATAM B2B trade), San Francisco (tech company global payouts), Los Angeles (Asia-Pacific trade).

**US Geographic Hotspots:** New York (BlackRock, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Ondo Finance), San Francisco (Anchorage Digital, Coinbase Institutional), Greenwich/Stamford CT (hedge funds), Chicago (CME-adjacent institutional crypto).

## 10. DIGITAL PAYMENT RAIL INTEGRATION

**Companies:** AlphaPoint, Conduit (SEPA/Fedwire/Pix/M-Pesa integrations), Alacriti, Bottomline Technologies, Modern Treasury, Fireblocks, BVNK, Circle, Fiserv (FIUSD for 10,000 FIs), SoFi

**Demand & Market Size:**
- Stablecoins emerging as the **interoperability "pipe" connecting isolated payment networks** — Fedwire, SEPA, M-Pesa, Pix, ACH, CHAPS all in one programmable layer
- B2B stablecoin payments grew 50x since 2023
- Dominant 2025–2026 model: regional banks/PSPs/fintechs adding stablecoin rails **without replatforming** via API

**Note:** Neither the Federal Reserve nor The Clearing House has announced direct stablecoin integration — creating significant opportunity for fintech intermediaries.

**USA Demand:** SoFi launched enterprise stablecoin (Dec 2025) for banking/fintech partners. Fiserv's FIUSD targets 10,000-institution client base.

**ICP:** Community banks and credit unions, regional banks seeking competitive differentiation, PSPs wanting 24/7 programmable settlement, B2B SaaS payment platforms, neobanks replacing SWIFT wires.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** New York (primary US banking regulatory center), Charlotte NC (BoA, Truist, Wells Fargo tech hubs), Chicago (Bottomline Technologies, B2B payment infrastructure), San Francisco (fintech API builders).

## 12. STABLECOIN AGENTIC PAYMENTS

**Companies (Protocol Layer):** Coinbase (x402 extension on A2A), Google (Agent Payments Protocol / AP2), Stripe + Tempo (Machine Payments Protocol / MPP), Anthropic (model providers), OpenAI, Base blockchain (Coinbase's L2)

**Demand & Market Size:**
- Emerging in 2026 — most nascent but highest-potential category
- Stablecoins positioned as the **"programmable funding layer" for AI agents**
- Machine-to-machine micropayments, fractionalized per-task billing, 24/7 autonomous transactions
- All three major protocols originate from US companies (Google, Coinbase, Stripe)
- Fenwick & West (2026): potentially the most transformative payment use case of the decade

**USA Demand:** US AI industry is the primary driver. AI agent frameworks needing payment infrastructure for autonomous tasks.

**ICP:** AI companies building autonomous agents, SaaS platforms embedding AI agents, developer platforms, IoT/embedded systems companies, e-commerce automation platforms, enterprise AI procurement tools.

**US Geographic Hotspots:** San Francisco/Silicon Valley (dominant — Google, Coinbase, Stripe, Anthropic, OpenAI all HQ'd here), New York (fintech AI applications), Seattle (Microsoft, Amazon agent infrastructure).
